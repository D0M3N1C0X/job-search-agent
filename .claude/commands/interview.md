Target: $ARGUMENTS

Read the archived posting (`profile/output/<company>_<role>/posting.txt`) and
the documents that were actually sent — prepare for the application that
exists, not a generic one.

Research the company for real: what they sell, how they make money, recent
public news, how the team you would join is structured, and anything about
their people practices that a candidate should know. Cite what you find.

Produce:

1. **The five questions most likely to come up**, given this posting and this
   CV — including the uncomfortable one about the biggest gap.
2. **STAR answers drawn only from the real history** in `profile.json`. Name
   the situation, the number, and the outcome. If there is no strong story for a
   likely question, say so and suggest the closest honest alternative.
3. **Three questions to ask them** that are specific to this company and
   demonstrably not answerable from their careers page.
4. **The salary conversation**: what `profile/answers.json` says, and how to
   handle the question if it comes early.
5. **Logistics**: stage, format, who is likely in the room, what to have open.

Keep it to one page a person can actually read the morning of.
