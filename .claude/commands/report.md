description: Rebuild the dashboard and read the funnel back
---

Run `python3 -m jsa stats` and `python3 -m jsa dashboard`. Mention that
`python3 -m jsa serve` opens the same view as an editable local app.

Then interpret it, with the honesty the numbers deserve:

- Where the funnel actually leaks — sourcing, submission rate, or reply rate.
- Which positioning track earns replies, and whether the difference is large
  enough to mean anything yet. With fewer than ~15 submissions per track, say
  the sample is too small rather than inventing a pattern.
- Which sources produce postings that score well, and which produce noise —
  that is watchlist feedback.
- Median days to reply, and what that implies for the follow-up horizon in
  `profile/profile.json`.

End with one change to make this week: a track to widen, companies to add, or a
habit to fix.
